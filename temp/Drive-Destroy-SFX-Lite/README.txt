DRIVE & DESTROY SFX - LITE (free sampler) by Rasterloom

27 sounds from Drive & Destroy SFX: one full engine (old sedan: idle, low, mid
and high RPM loops plus start-up), horn, gear shift, turbo, skid, brake, landing,
three cartoon zombie splats, a groan, crate smash, barrel explosion, explosion,
pistol, SMG burst, coin, cash bag, fuel can, combo step, survivor-rescued jingle,
roadblock fanfare and a 32-second seamless suburbs ambience loop.

WAV 48 kHz 16-bit, peak -3 dBFS. Loops (engine RPM layers, ambience) are seamless.
Smooth engine: play the four RPM loops together, crossfade their volumes by RPM
and bend their pitch - the sound sweeps from idle to redline.
